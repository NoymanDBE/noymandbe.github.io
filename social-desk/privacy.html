Noyman Social Desk

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

In short: Noyman Social Desk is a private tool that publishes Dror Ben Ephraim Noyman's own posts to his own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else. Apart from tagging people who have agreed to it, and showing him public comments, mentions and tags on his own accounts so that he can reply or moderate them, it does not handle other people's data. It never sells anyone's data or uses it for advertising, and passes nothing on except what it publishes to the platforms.

Who runs it

Noyman Social Desk ("the app", including its WhatsApp companion app "Noyman Desk Chat") is operated by Dror Ben Ephraim Noyman (Dror BE Noyman), an individual based in Israel, for his own professional social media accounts. Nobody else can sign in to it or use it.

What the app accesses, and why

Through the official APIs of Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and LinkedIn, the app accesses only accounts that belong to its operator:

  • his Facebook Page and his Instagram professional account, to publish posts, photos, videos, reels and stories that he has approved, together with his own comments on them (for example a first comment with links); to tag, mention or invite as collaborators other public accounts, only for people who have agreed to it; to read basic account details (name, ID, username) and publishing status; and to delete a post he asks to remove;
  • his LinkedIn profile, to read the basic profile details needed to post (name and member ID) and to publish posts he has approved, with his own comments on them.

If he uses the app's engagement features, the app also shows him public interactions on his own Page and Instagram account: comments on his posts (with the commenter's name or username, ID and, where the platform provides it, profile picture), posts and reviews that other people leave on his Page, and public posts, photos and comments that tag or @mention his Page or account. These are shown to him only so that he can read them, reply himself, and hide or delete a comment himself (for example spam or abuse). They are not stored, analysed, used for profiling or passed on.

What the app does not do

  • It does not store other people's personal data, except a short private list of the people who have agreed to be named or tagged in his posts (their name, public handle and when they agreed). Public comments, mentions and tags are used only as described above.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and does not use any data for advertising, profiling or analytics.
  • It does not read private messages on his Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn accounts. The only messages it reads are the ones its operator sends to it himself on WhatsApp (see below).
  • It does not post anything without the operator's approval.
  • Meta requires the business_management permission for this kind of app; the app uses it only to reach his own Page and Instagram account. It does not read the Page's follower list or the people in any business portfolio.

WhatsApp

The operator can send material to the app, and approve his posts, through a private WhatsApp chat. It runs on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform through the app "Noyman Desk Chat".

  • The chat answers only the operator's own phone number. Messages from any other number are ignored and not stored; at most a count and the last two digits of the number are logged, to spot misconfiguration.
  • What he sends there (text, photos, videos, voice notes, documents and links) is used only to prepare and publish his own posts. It is kept privately on his own computer or private server, and deleted once it is no longer needed for a post.
  • To draft and prepare posts, this material may be processed by an AI writing service (Anthropic's Claude API) and a speech-to-text service (ElevenLabs). They receive it only so that they can return their result to the app.
  • Messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp servers, under Meta's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Nothing is published until he approves it in the chat.

Storage and security

Access tokens are kept privately on the operator's own computer or private server, are never published or shared, and are used only to call the platforms' APIs over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Drafts stay private until he approves them; the images for an approved post are placed at a web address only so that the platform can fetch them, as Instagram's publishing API requires. If the app stops being used, its access tokens and any data obtained from the platforms are deleted.

Data deletion

Apart from that private list of people who agreed to appear in his posts, the app keeps no data about anyone except its operator. To end the app's access, remove it in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (on some layouts Apps and websites) → Remove, which also ends its Instagram access, or in LinkedIn under Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Other applications → Permitted services → Remove. Removing it on one platform revokes the app's access tokens for that platform.

If you believe the app holds any data about you, send a message to the Facebook Page Dr. Dror Ben Ephraim Noyman or a direct message to @dror.be.noyman on Instagram, and ask for it to be deleted. Any such data will be deleted, and the deletion confirmed to you, within 30 days.

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.
